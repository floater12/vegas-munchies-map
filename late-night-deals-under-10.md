## Legal basics (short)

Full wording, with citations, is on the [hub page](index.md). The short version, from official sources checked 2026-11-12:

- Retail cannabis is 21+. The CCB says it is illegal for anyone under 21 to buy, possess, or use retail cannabis, and that the only legal way to buy it is from a state-licensed retail store.
- You cannot use cannabis in any public place, in a moving vehicle (even as a passenger), or in an adult-use retail store. NRS 678D.310(3) makes consumption in a public place, in an adult-use cannabis retail store, or in a vehicle a misdemeanor, with a fine of not more than $600, except as provided for licensed consumption lounges.
- You may consume at a licensed cannabis consumption lounge.
- A property owner may prohibit cannabis on that property (NRS 678D.510(1)(c)). A casino or hotel can ban it on the property, including rooms. Do not consume at these cafes, in the casinos, or in a car in the lot.
- The CCB says it is illegal and dangerous to drive under the influence of cannabis.
