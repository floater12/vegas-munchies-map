## Legal basics (short)

Full wording, with citations, is on the [hub page](index.md). The short version, from the [CCB Laws & Regulations page](https://ccb.nv.gov/laws-regulations/) and [NRS Chapter 678D](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/NRS/NRS-678D.html), both checked 2026-11-13:

- Retail cannabis is 21+. The CCB says it is illegal for anyone under 21 to buy, possess, or use retail cannabis, and that the only legal way to buy it is from a state-licensed retail store.
- You cannot use cannabis in any public place, in a moving vehicle (even as a passenger), or in an adult-use retail store. NRS 678D.310(3) makes consumption in a public place, in an adult-use cannabis retail store, or in a vehicle a misdemeanor, with a fine of not more than $600, except as provided for licensed consumption lounges.
- You may consume at a licensed cannabis consumption lounge. There is no CCB-listed lounge on the east side on the list fetched today.
- A property owner may prohibit cannabis on that property (NRS 678D.510(1)(c)). A casino or hotel can ban it on the property, including rooms. Do not consume at Boulder Station, at Arizona Charlie's, in a parking lot, or in a car.
- The CCB says it is illegal and dangerous to drive under the influence of cannabis.
- The CCB also says: "Check with local jurisdictions to find out how local laws may affect you."
