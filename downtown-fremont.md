## How to use this page

There is no CCB-licensed retail dispensary on Fremont Street itself, and none with an address on the Fremont Street Experience pedestrian mall. The same 1,500-foot rule that keeps licensed shops off the casino-lined Strip applies downtown. In a county of 100,000 or more, an adult-use cannabis establishment may not be located "within 1,500 feet of an establishment that holds a nonrestricted gaming license described in subsection 1 or 2 of NRS 463.0177 and that existed on the date on which the application for the proposed adult-use cannabis establishment was submitted to the Board." That distance "must be measured from the front door of the proposed adult-use cannabis establishment to the closest point of the property line of a school, community facility or gaming establishment" (NRS 678B.250, fetched 2026-11-12). The closest licensed shops are a short drive south and west of Fremont. They are listed below.

Start with a CCB-licensed store if you need to buy cannabis. Drive times below are uncongested OSRM driving estimates from 200 Fremont St, Las Vegas, NV 89101, not a promise about traffic. Re-check hours and the CCB list before you go. Do not consume cannabis on Fremont Street, in a casino, in a hotel, in a parking lot, or in a car.

No CCB-licensed consumption lounge sits in this downtown cluster on the list fetched today. The three lounges on that list (DAZED!, SOCIETY, and SMOKE AND MIRRORS) are farther south, toward Desert Inn, Paradise, and Sammy Davis Jr Drive, and are covered on the [Strip-adjacent page](strip-adjacent.md).

## Legal basics (short)

Full wording, with citations, is on the [hub page](index.md). The short version, from official sources checked 2026-11-12:

- Retail cannabis is 21+. The CCB says it is illegal for anyone under 21 to buy, possess, or use retail cannabis, and that the only legal way to buy it is from a state-licensed retail store.
- You cannot use cannabis in any public place, in a moving vehicle (even as a passenger), or in an adult-use retail store. NRS 678D.310(3) makes consumption in a public place, in an adult-use cannabis retail store, or in a vehicle a misdemeanor, with a fine of not more than $600, except as provided for licensed consumption lounges.
- You may consume at a licensed cannabis consumption lounge. There is no CCB-listed lounge in this downtown cluster on the list fetched today.
- A property owner may prohibit cannabis on that property (NRS 678D.510(1)(c)). A casino or hotel can ban it on the property, including rooms. Do not consume on Fremont Street, in a casino, in a hotel, or in a car in the lot.
- The CCB says it is illegal and dangerous to drive under the influence of cannabis.
- The 1,500-foot nonrestricted-gaming setback in NRS 678B.250 is why there is no licensed retail shop on Fremont Street itself.
