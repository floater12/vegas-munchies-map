This page is part of a Las Vegas munchies map. It is for adults 21 and older. It is not legal advice. You cannot use cannabis in a public place, in a vehicle, or in a retail store. Casino restaurants and hotel properties are places to eat or stay, not consumption lounges.

## How to use this page

There is no CCB-licensed retail dispensary on the casino-lined Strip itself. Nevada law is why. In a county of 100,000 or more, an adult-use cannabis establishment may not be located "within 1,500 feet of an establishment that holds a nonrestricted gaming license described in subsection 1 or 2 of NRS 463.0177 and that existed on the date on which the application for the proposed adult-use cannabis establishment was submitted to the Board." That distance "must be measured from the front door of the proposed adult-use cannabis establishment to the closest point of the property line of a school, community facility or gaming establishment" (NRS 678B.250, fetched 2026-11-12). The closest licensed shops are off the boulevard, a short drive away. They are listed below.

Start with a CCB-licensed store if you need to buy cannabis, or a licensed lounge if you want to consume on site. Then go to a restaurant if you want late-night food. Drive times below are uncongested OSRM driving estimates from mid-Strip at 3600 S Las Vegas Blvd (the official Bellagio / CityCenter address), not a promise about traffic. Re-check hours and the CCB list before you go. Do not consume cannabis in a restaurant, in a casino, in a hotel, in a parking lot, or in a car.

A retail consumption lounge attached to a store that was already in compliance is not subject to that same 1,500-foot gaming setback, but it still may not be on airport property (NRS 678B.322). Even with that lounge exception, the CCB list fetched today still has no lounge with a Las Vegas Boulevard address.

Every store and lounge below is on the [CCB List of Licensees](https://ccb.nv.gov/list-of-licensees/) fetched 2026-11-12 (the Board marks that list "Current as of 10/02/2026"). Addresses, license numbers, adult-use status, and the delivery flag come from that list. Hours are included only when the business's own official page was fetched today. Distances are driving routes from 3600 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109, using the U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder (Public_AR_Current) and OSRM driving, run 2026-11-12.

Planet 13's visit page also advertises a free shuttle to and from 15 Strip hotels (13 riders a trip; hours vary; book at (702) 500-0154) and says delivery is to residential addresses only, never hotels or casinos, daily 9 AM to 11:45 PM. The Grove store at 4647 University Center Dr is on the CCB list (license 19619496668049413129, delivery Y) but is not given a drive time below. See "Dropped."

## Legal basics (short)

Full wording, with citations, is on the [hub page](index.md). The short version, from official sources checked 2026-11-12:

- Retail cannabis is 21+. The CCB says it is illegal for anyone under 21 to buy, possess, or use retail cannabis, and that the only legal way to buy it is from a state-licensed retail store.
- You cannot use cannabis in any public place, in a moving vehicle (even as a passenger), or in an adult-use retail store. NRS 678D.310(3) makes consumption in a public place, in an adult-use cannabis retail store, or in a vehicle a misdemeanor, with a fine of not more than $600, except as provided for licensed consumption lounges.
- You may consume at a licensed cannabis consumption lounge. The three lounges on the CCB list fetched today are DAZED!, SOCIETY, and SMOKE AND MIRRORS, all listed above.
- A property owner may prohibit cannabis on that property (NRS 678D.510(1)(c)). A casino or hotel can ban it on the property, including rooms. Do not consume on the Strip, in a casino restaurant, in a hotel, or in a car in the lot.
- The CCB says it is illegal and dangerous to drive under the influence of cannabis.
- The 1,500-foot nonrestricted-gaming setback in NRS 678B.250, and the lounge exception in NRS 678B.322, are why there is no licensed retail shop on the casino-lined Strip and why the closest real shops are the off-boulevard addresses above.
