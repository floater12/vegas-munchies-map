# Open at 2 AM

**Last verified:** 2026-11-12 (America/Los_Angeles, PST)

**Area / topic:** Every late-night food spot and CCB-licensed dispensary on this directory whose official hours say it is open 24 hours or past 2 AM, grouped by area.

This page is part of a Las Vegas munchies map. It is for adults 21 and older. It is not legal advice. You cannot use cannabis in a public place, in a vehicle, or in a retail store. A 24-hour cafe or a 24-hour shop is not a consumption lounge.

## How to use this page

Each row below carries the hours exactly as the business's own official page published them on 2026-11-12, and a link to that page. "Open 24 hours" means the page says so. "Until 3:00 AM" or "until 2:00 AM" means the page gives a closing time; a place that closes at 2:00 AM is not open at 2:00 AM, so those are listed separately. Hours change, and holiday hours can differ (Coronado Cafe, for example, lists shorter hours on Thanksgiving and Christmas). Re-check the official page before you go. Every dispensary is on the [CCB List of Licensees](https://ccb.nv.gov/list-of-licensees/) fetched 2026-11-12 (marked "Current as of 10/02/2026"), and every address and license number is on that area's page.

Do not consume cannabis at a restaurant, in a casino, in a hotel, in a parking lot, or in a car. A 24-hour drive-thru or curbside window is for buying, not for using.

## Legal basics (short)

Full wording, with citations, is on the [hub page](index.md). The short version, from official sources checked 2026-11-12:

- Retail cannabis is 21+. The CCB says it is illegal for anyone under 21 to buy, possess, or use retail cannabis, and that the only legal way to buy it is from a state-licensed retail store.
- You cannot use cannabis in any public place, in a moving vehicle (even as a passenger), or in an adult-use retail store. NRS 678D.310(3) makes consumption in a public place, in an adult-use cannabis retail store, or in a vehicle a misdemeanor, with a fine of not more than $600, except as provided for licensed consumption lounges.
- You may consume at a licensed cannabis consumption lounge.
- A property owner may prohibit cannabis on that property (NRS 678D.510(1)(c)). A casino or hotel can ban it on the property, including rooms. Do not consume at a late-night restaurant, in a casino, in a hotel, in a parking lot, or in a car.
- The CCB says it is illegal and dangerous to drive under the influence of cannabis.
- The CCB also says: "Check with local jurisdictions to find out how local laws may affect you."
