# Las Vegas munchies map

**Status:** Draft — queued for Ashley's OK. Do not treat this as published.

**Last verified:** 2026-11-11 (America/Los_Angeles, PST)

This is a same-day-sourced directory of two things that are often asked about together in Las Vegas: real licensed dispensaries (and the few licensed consumption lounges), and nearby late-night food, including casino graveyard specials.

It is for adults 21 and older. It is not legal advice, and it is not a guide to consuming cannabis in public, in a car, in a casino, or in a hotel.

## How it works

- Every dispensary or lounge on these pages was checked against the Nevada Cannabis Compliance Board licensee list. If a shop is not on that list, it is not listed here as a licensed store. Tribal establishments are not licensed by the CCB and are not on that list, so they are not listed here.
- The CCB page fetched on 2026-11-11 is marked "Current as of 10/02/2026." Both dates are noted whenever that list is used. License status can change; re-check the list before relying on an older page.
- Every food price and every food hour comes from an official restaurant or casino page fetched on the page's "last verified" date. Social posts and old menu PDFs are not good enough when they disagree with a current official page.
- Distances, when shown, come from the U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder plus an OSRM driving route run that day. If a distance could not be verified, it is omitted.
- Pages are drafted and then queued for Ashley's OK before they are treated as published.

## Legal basics (official wording)

The wording below was checked on 2026-11-11 from the [CCB Laws & Regulations page](https://ccb.nv.gov/laws-regulations/) and [NRS Chapter 678D](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/NRS/NRS-678D.html). Local rules can be stricter. The CCB page also says: "Check with local jurisdictions to find out how local laws may affect you."

**Buying and age.** The CCB page states: "The only legal way to buy cannabis in Nevada is from a state-licensed retail store. It is illegal to buy cannabis from any other source. Customers purchasing retail cannabis will have to show proper identification proving they are 21 or older, just like for alcohol purchases." It also states: "It is illegal for anyone under the age of 21 to buy, possess, or use retail cannabis."

**Where you may not consume.** The CCB page states: "You cannot use cannabis in any public place." "You cannot use cannabis in a moving vehicle, even if you're a passenger."

NRS 678D.310(3) states: "Except as otherwise provided in subsection 10 or by regulations adopted by the Board pursuant to NRS 678B.645, a person who smokes or otherwise consumes cannabis or a cannabis product in a public place, in an adult-use cannabis retail store or in a vehicle is guilty of a misdemeanor punished by a fine of not more than $600."

That covers casino floors and other places open to the public. It also means you may not consume on the sales floor of a licensed retail store.

**Where you may consume.** The CCB page states: "Cannabis can be consumed on private property (at home, for example), and as long as the property owner has not prohibited it." "Cannabis may be consumed at a licensed cannabis consumption lounge."

NRS 678D.310(10) states: "A person may smoke or otherwise consume cannabis or a cannabis product in a cannabis consumption lounge."

**Casinos and hotels.** The official sources fetched on 2026-11-11 do not add a separate sentence that says "hotel rooms are illegal under state law." They do ban consumption in a public place, in a retail store, and in a vehicle, and they let a property owner prohibit cannabis on that property. NRS 678D.510(1)(c) says this chapter does not prohibit "a person who occupies, owns or controls a privately owned property from prohibiting or otherwise restricting the smoking, cultivation, processing, manufacture, sale, delivery or transfer of cannabis on that property." A hotel or casino can ban cannabis on its property, including rooms. This directory will not tell anyone to consume in a casino or a hotel.

**Driving.** The CCB page states: "It is illegal and dangerous to drive under the influence of cannabis."

On 2026-11-11 the CCB list named three licensed consumption lounges, all Adult Use, none marked for delivery: DAZED! at 2548 W Desert Inn Rd; SOCIETY at 4640 Paradise Rd Ste 8-9; and SMOKE AND MIRRORS at 2975 Sammy Davis Jr Dr. Those addresses will be re-checked on any page that lists them.

## Dropped from this hub

- A blanket line that "consumption in hotels is illegal under Nevada law," because the official pages and NRS text fetched on 2026-11-11 do not say that in those words. The public-place rule and the property-owner rule are what they do say, and that is what this page uses.
- The CCB page's citation of this property-owner language as "NRS 678D.500." In the current NRS text fetched from the Legislature site on 2026-11-11, that language is in NRS 678D.510. This hub cites 678D.510.
